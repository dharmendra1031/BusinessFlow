ENQUIRY DATA

How we use project enquiry details

When you submit a project enquiry, you provide contact information, business context and a description of your proposed software. Resmoh IT uses this information to review the request, contact you and prepare a potential project discussion or proposal.

What we collect

Your name, phone number, optional email and company details, chosen service, business location, estimated budget, timeline and project description. We recommend that you do not send passwords, payment card information or confidential customer records in this form.

How the request is handled

Submission information is sent securely to the internal BusinessFlow lead-management system, where authorized staff may review it for project qualification and follow-up. Sending a request does not create a contract or purchase obligation.

Enquiry tracking in this browser

After successful submission, the website may save a short-lived, signed, HttpOnly browser receipt for up to 14 days. This allows a read-only status check of the latest request in the same browser without displaying contact details or commercial documents. Clearing cookies or switching devices removes this access.

Before public launch

This initial enquiry notice describes the purpose of the form. Resmoh IT must finalize its legal privacy policy, retention periods, data-subject request contact details, third-party service disclosures and applicable jurisdiction requirements before putting this website into production.
